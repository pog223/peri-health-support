Privacy Policy

Peri Health - Perimenopause Tracker
Last updated: May 8, 2026

Your privacy is foundational to Peri Health. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

Data you enter

Symptom logs are stored locally on your device using Apple encrypted storage. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share it.

Apple Health data

If you connect Apple Health, Peri Health reads sleep duration and heart rate solely to pre-fill your daily log. We do not transmit Apple Health data to any server. You can revoke permission in iOS Settings - Health - Data Access and Devices.

AI analysis

When you request AI insights, a statistical summary of your symptoms (averages, frequency counts) is sent to Anthropic Claude API over an encrypted connection. Raw log entries and personal identifiers are never sent. Anthropic data handling is governed by their Privacy Policy at anthropic.com.

Subscription data

Payment is processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive or store your credit card number or billing address.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Your name, email address, or phone number (no account required)
  • Device location or GPS coordinates
  • Contacts, camera roll, or microphone access
  • Advertising identifiers or behavioral tracking data

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party for marketing purposes. The only external service that receives data is Anthropic Claude API (statistical summaries only), and only when you actively request an AI feature.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

All symptom data is stored on your device. Deleting the Peri Health app permanently removes all locally stored data. There is no server-side account to delete.

5. Security

Data stored on your device is protected by iOS data protection encryption. Data in transit to the Claude API uses TLS 1.2 or higher.

6. Children's Privacy

Peri Health is designed for adults experiencing perimenopause. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under the age of 13.
