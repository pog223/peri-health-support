FAQ

How do I connect Apple Health?

Go to the Profile tab and tap Connect Apple Health. You can revoke this in iOS Settings > Health > Data Access.

Is my data private?

Yes. All logs are stored only on your device. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Settings app > Apple ID > Subscriptions > Peri Health > Cancel Subscription.
